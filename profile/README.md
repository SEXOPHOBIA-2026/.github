**SEXOPHOBIA 🔞** is an adult, Casual, Indie, Simulation video game. Developed and published by **Lewd Star**. It was released on August 2026 for Windows.

## What is SEXOPHOBIA 🔞?

A dating sim and card-based social strategy game where everyday life collides with the unknown. Build relationships, craft your deck, and uncover a hidden reality where every connection shapes your path—and your fate. Some inherit wealth. Some inherit problems. In this dating sim and card-based social strategy game, a quiet move to a suburban house quickly turns into something far less ordinary. Strange encounters, subtle shifts in reality, and a mysterious grimoire hint that there’s more beneath the surface than meets the eye.

In SEXOPHOBIA 🔞, you step into a story where everyday life blends with the unknown. The people you meet aren’t always what they seem, and the connections you build carry weight beyond simple conversation. Every interaction matters. Every choice shapes your path. Engage with characters through a unique card-based interaction system, where your deck defines your approach. Experiment with different combinations, adapt to each personality, and discover what works — and what doesn’t. Balance your time between exploring the city by day and diving into more intimate, unpredictable encounters by night. As new opportunities emerge, so do new questions. What is really happening around you? And how far are you willing to go to understand it?

## 💠Key Features

- 🃏 Card-Based Interaction System — build and refine your deck to influence every encounter
- 💞 Relationship-Driven Progression — connections unlock new paths and possibilities
- 🌆 Day/Night Cycle — different gameplay layers and opportunities
- 🎴 Deck Building & Strategy — discover, collect, and combine cards
- 🎭 Distinct Characters — each with unique behavior and reactions
- 🔮 Modern Setting with a Mystical Edge — reality hides more than it shows
- ♻️ High Replayability — multiple approaches, outcomes, and discoveries
